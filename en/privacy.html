1. Scope

This policy applies to the Resumate desktop app for macOS and Windows (the “App”) and its introductory website (the “Website”), both distributed by KWAK TAEHOON (the “Operator”). The Operator complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws, and this policy describes how the App actually behaves.

2. Information the operator collects

The Operator does not collect personal information from App users.

  • The App has no Operator account or sign-up.
  • The Operator does not run any server that receives your resumes, conversations, uploaded files, voice, or interview records. The App only communicates with the destinations listed in Section 4.
  • The App has no feature that sends usage statistics, error reports, or advertising identifiers to the Operator.

If you contact the Operator by email, the Operator receives your email address and the content of your message in order to reply. This information is used only to handle your inquiry and is deleted without delay once it is resolved. Unless you ask for deletion, it may be kept for up to one year to help with recurring issues.

If you consent to Website analytics, Google Analytics collects visit statistics. See the Website analytics notice for data and refusal controls.

When you choose a folder to analyze, the AI is blocked from reading secret files such as .env, key files, and .ssh, and from following links that point outside the folder. Your entire home folder and credential folders cannot be chosen as analysis folders.

Signing in, storing and refreshing credentials, and sending AI requests are handled by Anthropic’s official Claude Code. Resumate never receives Claude sign-in codes, tokens, or cookies and does not read credential files; it only checks whether you are signed in. Information sent to Anthropic, and anything Claude Code collects on its own, is governed by the terms between you and Anthropic and by the Anthropic Privacy Policy.

Website

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no external fonts. Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics information is sent before you consent to optional Website analytics. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Download files are also served by GitHub, which receives connection information when you request a download. GitHub’s handling and retention of this information are governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Optional Website analytics and overseas processing

This notice was added on November 7, 2026. Website analytics starts only after you select “Allow analytics”. Refusing or withdrawing consent does not affect the Website or downloads.

  • Provider and purpose: Google LLC and Google Analytics service affiliates process Website traffic, usage and download-click statistics. See Google’s privacy policy and contact information.
  • Data: random cookie identifiers, visit times, page URLs and titles, referring site domains, browser, operating system and device information, approximate location, clicks to installation instructions and installer downloads, and the selected OS. Network requests expose your IP address to Google; Google states that GA4 does not log or store individual IP addresses.
  • Excluded: resumes, cover letters, conversations, files, recordings and App contact details are not sent. The tag removes page query strings and fragments and referrer paths. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled.
  • Countries, timing and method: after consent, your browser sends information over HTTPS on page visits and clicks. Processing may take place in the United States and other Google data center countries; no single storage country is specified.
  • Retention: GA4 user- and event-level retention is set to 2 months. Aggregated reports may remain separately. Analytics cookies last up to 60 days from creation. Your choice stays in this browser’s site storage until you change it or clear that storage.
  • Refusal and withdrawal: select “Decline” under “Analytics preferences” in the footer to stop subsequent analytics and delete this site’s analytics cookies. This does not automatically erase data already sent; contact the privacy officer below for related requests. You can also clear browser site data or use the Google Analytics opt-out tool.

5. Purposes of processing

Information the App stores on your computer or sends elsewhere is used only to:

  • Provide the features you request, such as resume diagnosis and editing, project achievement summaries, tailored resumes and cover letters, and interview questions and answer feedback
  • Keep your work so you can continue later
  • Check for and install App updates
  • Reply to your inquiries (if you contact the Operator by email)
  • Analyze Website traffic and download clicks and improve the site, only with the visitor’s optional consent

6. Retention

  • Information on your computer: remains on your computer until you delete it in the App or remove the data folders. The Operator does not hold it.
  • Information sent to Anthropic: retained for the period set by Anthropic’s privacy policy and your account settings.
  • Inquiry emails: retained for the period described in Section 2.
  • Optional Website analytics: follows the periods and controls in Section 4.

7. Third-party provision and outsourcing

The Operator does not outsource the processing of App resumes, conversations or files. Google Analytics processes optional Website analytics after visitor consent; the provider, purpose, data and retention are described in Section 4.

When you use AI features, the transfer to Anthropic is not a provision by the Operator; it is your own direct use of Anthropic’s Claude Code service under your own account. Resumate only prepares the request on your computer and hands it to Claude Code.

9. Device permissions

  • Microphone: used only when you record a spoken answer during interview practice with Echo. Each recording is limited to five minutes and is transcribed on your computer by an on-device speech recognition model (Whisper). The audio itself is never sent out; only the transcript is included in AI requests. Text-based practice works without this permission.
  • Files and folders: the App reads only files and folders you choose or drag in.

The App does not request location, camera, contacts, or system notification permissions.

10. Your rights

You may at any time access, correct, or delete your personal information and request that its processing be suspended.

  • Information on your computer: the Operator does not hold it, so you can view, edit, and delete it directly in the App. See Section 11 to remove everything.
  • Information sent to Anthropic: contact Anthropic directly as described in the Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • Inquiry emails: request deletion at taehoon8206@gmail.com and it will be handled without delay.

You may also exercise these rights through a legal representative or an authorized agent.

11. Deletion

Inquiry email is deleted irrecoverably when its retention period ends. Retention and withdrawal for optional Website analytics are described in Section 4.

You can delete information on your computer yourself. Individual resumes and archive files can be deleted in the App. To remove everything, quit the App and follow the steps below. Uninstalling the App alone may not remove the data folders.

macOS

  1. Move Resumate from the Applications folder to the Trash.
  2. In Finder, choose Go → Go to Folder and delete each of these folders:
    • ~/Library/Application Support/com.resumate.app
    • ~/Library/Application Support/resumate
    • ~/Library/WebKit/com.resumate.app
    • ~/Library/Caches/com.resumate.app
    • ~/.resumate
  3. Empty the Trash.

Windows

  1. Uninstall Resumate from Settings → Apps.
  2. Enter each of these paths in the File Explorer address bar and delete the folder:
    • %APPDATA%\com.resumate.app
    • %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.resumate.app
    • %USERPROFILE%\.resumate

Files exported to your Downloads folder must be deleted separately. If you do not use Claude Code for anything else, follow Anthropic’s instructions to sign out of or remove it. Signing out of Claude Code may also sign out other tools that use the same account.

12. Security measures

  • On macOS, the App data folder and archive folder are restricted so that only the current user can access them.
  • The App’s internal communication stays on the same computer (127.0.0.1) and requires an authentication value generated fresh at each launch.
  • Temporary files for PDF and voice processing are readable only by the current user and are deleted after processing.
  • Tool permissions prevent the AI from reading secret files inside an analysis folder, and the AI is not given permission to modify files or run commands.
  • Updates are installed only after their signatures are verified.
  • When Claude Code is installed through the App, it is downloaded from the official distribution and its checksum is verified.

Information on your computer is not separately encrypted by the App. We recommend using your operating system’s disk encryption (macOS FileVault, Windows BitLocker, etc.) together with a locked user account.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

The App uses no analytics cookies. The Website uses Google Analytics cookies named _ga and starting with _ga_ only after consent. Your choice is saved in site storage and can be changed under “Analytics preferences” in the footer. App storage used to remember screen state stays on your computer.

14. Children

The App is intended for adults preparing for employment and is not directed at children under 14. The Operator does not collect children’s personal information. The Claude Code account required for AI features is subject to the age requirements set by Anthropic.
